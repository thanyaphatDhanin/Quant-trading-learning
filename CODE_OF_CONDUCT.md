# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Quant Trading Learning is committed to providing a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

We welcome contributors of all experience levels who share an interest in quantitative trading, financial research, and open education.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior includes:

- Communicating respectfully and professionally.
- Providing constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Acknowledging mistakes, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Giving appropriate credit to original sources and contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting another person's work.
- Repeated disruptive behavior or deliberate abuse of project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official community channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing violations. Depending on the severity of the incident, they may issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or remove a contributor from the project.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository or organization profile.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.

Reference: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
